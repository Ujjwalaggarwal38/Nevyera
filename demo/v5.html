On being new

Nevyera is a few months old. We have two products running, no customer logos to show you, and no intention of borrowing anybody else's.

That's an awkward thing to lead with on a website. The convention is to put up a row of grey company marks and a quote from a Director of Operations who may or may not exist. We'd rather tell you what we actually are and let you decide.

What we are is two working products and a small team that answers its own phone. The Lead Manager is built on Meta's official WhatsApp Business API, which matters because the unofficial route eventually gets business numbers banned. Inventory handles stock by QR across warehouses, runs vendor bidding on purchases, and bills through Zoho.

If you need something neither of them does, that's the third thing we sell — the same people, building to your process rather than ours.

Four things you can check.

Not claims. Things you could verify this afternoon without asking us.

01

Official WhatsApp Business API

Meta's Cloud API, not a browser automation workaround. The unofficial route works until it doesn't, and then your business number is gone. Ask any vendor which one they use.

02

A team in India you can phone

Support is a person in your timezone who knows the product. There's no ticket queue because there aren't enough of us to need one.

03

Your data leaves when you do

Export leads, stock and conversations whenever you want, in a format you can open. We'll say that out loud because most contracts don't.

04

Running this afternoon

Connect your number, add your team, begin. No implementation project, no consultant, no three-month rollout that quietly becomes six.

There are no testimonials on this page. We don't have customers yet. Inventing quotes from businesses that don't exist would be a strange way to ask you to trust us with yours. When they're real they'll be here, with names and numbers you can ring.
